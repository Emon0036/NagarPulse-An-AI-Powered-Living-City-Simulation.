1. A* Search Pathfinding

Navigates citizens between Town Hall, Workplaces, Bazaar Stalls, Bank Vault, and Academies avoiding structures and obstacles.

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)
h(n) = |x1 - x2| + |y1 - y2| (Manhattan)
  • Optimal coordinate path generation
  • Dynamic rerouting on congestion
  • 2.5D Isometric screen translation

2. Constraint Satisfaction (CSP)

Matches unemployed or seeking villagers into industry vacancies under hard real-world constraints.

Variables: V = {j_1, ..., j_m}
Domain: D(j) = {a | a.edu ≥ j.edu, skills ∩ j.req ≠ ∅}
Heuristic: Minimum Remaining Values (MRV)
  • Reservation wage constraint satisfaction
  • Workplace capacity limit enforcement
  • Forward checking constraint propagation

3. Logistic Regression ML Model

P(Distress) = 1 / (1 + e^-(W^T X + b))

Proposal Slide 11 Scenario: Simulating a 30% Food Price Shock

When food prices increase by 30%, economic shifts do not affect everyone equally. The AI simulation models the four micro and macro ripple effects:

Low-Income Citizens

Slashed discretionary spending down to 20% to absorb essential living costs.

Businesses & Retail

Adjust prices across related goods, shifting local village market equilibrium.

Household Savings

Savings accumulation stalls; vulnerable families withdraw safety buffers.

Economic Indicators

Gini coefficient spikes, reflecting widening inequality between income tiers.
